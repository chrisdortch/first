# Using Clover Today

The current safe operating mode is read-only truth gathering plus preview-only preparation.

## Morning

Say:

> Use CloverApps to tell me what I should know today across my current priorities, grounded in current sources and without changing anything.

Expected result: one primary item, up to two secondary items, source freshness, important unknowns, and any genuine owner decision.

## Understand

Say:

> Use CloverApps to explain why the most important item matters, show the evidence, uncertainty, consequences, and what could change the recommendation.

## Choose the next move

Say:

> Use CloverApps to recommend the smallest highest-value next step, considering deadlines, risk, cash, owner attention, and project dependencies.

## Let Clover work safely

Say:

> Use CloverApps to do only the safe, reversible, preview-only parts, preserve rollback, and stop before any approval-gated action.

Safe parts normally include current readback, research, organizing, drafting, schemas, tests, isolated branches, synthetic rehearsals, previews, and receipts. They do not include production deployment, merge, private-data movement, secret reveal, purchases, messages, domain changes, or OpenAI Site changes unless separately and exactly approved.

## Close the day

Say:

> Use CloverApps to report what changed, what was verified, what remains unknown, what authority was used, and what should be recorded in today's log.

## One-decision mode

When Clover genuinely needs Chris, it should present one compact Action Card:

- what needs approval;
- why it matters now;
- exact scope;
- expected result;
- risk and rollback;
- cost;
- expiration;
- Approve, Reject, Change, or Tell me why.

No approval should be interpreted more broadly than the exact card.
