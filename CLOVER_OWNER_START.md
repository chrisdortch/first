# Clover Owner Start

This is the owner operating guide for the Clover Core trunk-activation candidate. It is not a ratification, merge approval, production approval, or grant of standing authority.

## Start here

For this candidate gate, begin in the private ChatGPT Project with the exact candidate Context Gateway connection or the exact source-bound candidate preview. Verify the Gateway source identity before relying on it, then ask one compact question:

```text
Use Clover Core.
Target: [target]
Outcome: [outcome]
Mode: [mode]
```

`Target` names one project, branch, decision, or bounded portfolio question. `Outcome` says what finished evidence should prove. Replace `[mode]` with a ceiling such as `read-only`, `draft-only`, `preview-only`, or `production proposal`; it is not authority to perform every action within it. A production proposal prepares an exact owner decision; it does not authorize production.

The owner window should answer five questions:

1. What should I know?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What do you recommend?
4. Do the safe parts.
5. What happened?

## Minimum useful answer

Require the response to show:

- the dated source-bound facts and any inference separately;
- live, current, candidate, historical, unavailable, and unknown states without collapsing them;
- broad-mission, owner-usable, live-production, and verified-candidate completion with scope, method, confidence, freshness, and exclusions;
- no more than three priorities and one recommended next action;
- the exact source, target, connector plan, rollback anchor, and cost lane;
- the Action ID and durable envelope when execution is proposed;
- the owner decision still required; and
- the final receipt or an explicit reason no receipt exists.

The August 20 status at `portfolio/status/candidates/2026-08-20/status.json`, Registry 2.0 projection, Today session, and Handoff Ledger are candidates. They do not replace the canonical v1 status or registry merely because they are available.

CloverApps remains the intended protected owner window. Live CloverApps 7.4.2 and the saved Sites 81 candidate checkpoint remain separate and unchanged in this gate; no integrated CloverApps owner-window preview or live experience is claimed.

## Decision and execution loop

1. Use ChatGPT with Clover Core to observe, understand, and recommend from the minimum necessary sources.
2. Review the proposed Action ID, exact envelope version and hash, source and target identities, expiry, operations, stop conditions, rollback, cost, and required connectors.
3. Give a separate exact approval only if the envelope is accurate. Require the owner console to append an immutable approval attestation that binds the Action ID and envelope hash to the exact candidate index. Conversational agreement, an older approval, or this guide is not approval; if the approval record cannot be written and verified under a separate candidate-branch authorization, the action stays pending.
4. After that exact approval record is visible in the Handoff index, give Codex exactly:

   ```text
   Use CloverApps to execute approved Action ID [ID].
   ```

5. Require Codex to refresh material facts, stay inside the envelope, verify independent readback, and return a source-bound execution receipt.
6. Return the receipt to ChatGPT for independent review before any later merge or production decision.

If the owner session, candidate status, Handoff Ledger, connector, exact source, rollback, or approval binding is unavailable or contradictory, stop. Label the fact `unknown`; never substitute memory or a different version.

## Authority boundary

No Clover component has standing authority to merge, deploy or promote production, access production or private data, reveal or mutate secrets, send messages, spend money, change domains or aliases, alter permissions or persistent environment settings, exercise payments, or modify an OpenAI Site. Each such operation requires its own exact owner authorization and an execution lane capable of enforcing it.
